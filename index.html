Key interpretation

The competitors demonstrate that autonomous UGVs are already moving from laboratory concepts toward field-oriented systems. The differentiation opportunity is therefore not “autonomous UGV” itself — it is the combination of mission, vehicle scale, autonomy progression, terrain context and human control philosophy.

04 / The open space

Where VYOM R1 is intentionally different

POSITIONING
01

Mission-first, not platform-first

VYOM R1 is being shaped around last-mile tactical logistics in rough and narrow terrain, rather than treating logistics as only one possible attachment.

02

Progressive autonomy

The system is structured around four operating modes — Manual, Follow-Me, Supervised Autonomous and Autonomous Waypoint — allowing autonomy to increase with operator confidence.

03

Human override remains central

Autonomy is designed as an assistive layer. The operator can retain control when terrain, mission conditions or perception uncertainty require intervention.

04

Compact Indian deployment context

The target is a compact UGV suited to difficult, constrained routes and Indian operational conditions, with a pathway toward institutional trials.

Research conclusion

VYOM R1 is not positioned as the first autonomous UGV. Its proposed differentiation is the combination of a compact form factor, tactical last-mile logistics mission, progressive autonomy, human override and an Indian deployment context.
